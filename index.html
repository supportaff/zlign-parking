How it works

Two sides, one empty bay

A space owner turns a spare parking spot into a steady rental. A vehicle owner gets a reliable place to park close by, without hunting for one every day.

If you have a space

List a driveway, garage or bay and set a weekly or monthly rate.

  • ✓ Add spot photos and an ID proof so vehicle owners know it is real
  • ✓ Block dates on your owner calendar whenever the bay is taken
  • ✓ Approve or decline each booking request — you always get the final say
  • ✓ See upcoming, active and completed bookings in booking history

If you need a space

Find a bay near you on the map, request the dates, and park.

  • ✓ See free bays over the next 7 days on the map before you book
  • ✓ Get alerts when a bay you watch may become free
  • ✓ Renew with one tap, with a reminder two days before the booking ends
  • ✓ Keep your vehicle details in My Garage, including a photo of the plate

Built in

Everything the handover needs

Booking, approval, contact and renewal live in the app. Payment happens between the two of you, at the spot.

📍

OpenStreetMap availability

Pins show how many bays are free over the next 7 days, based on every listing on Zlign — not just your own bookings.

✅

Owner approval in 24 hours

Every booking is a request. The owner approves or declines; unanswered requests expire after a day and release the bay.

🔒

Private profiles

Your profile is readable only by you. Contact happens inside the booking, using the phone number attached to it.

💬

Chat templates

Quick in-app messages for the usual questions, or share the same message on WhatsApp in one tap.

⭐

One review per booking

Only the vehicle owner on a completed booking can review it, and only once. Ratings reflect real stays.

🛡️

Admin moderation

Listings need photos and ID proof, past start times are blocked, and listings with active bookings cannot be deleted.

Where it stands

Honest release status

Zlign is in closed testing on Android. Here is exactly what is live, what is waiting on Google, and what is already built for the next update.

Weekly and monthly only

Hourly and daily bookings were removed from new bookings, so a bay is rented for a real stretch of time rather than an afternoon. Existing hourly and daily bookings still load.

You pay the owner, not the app

Zlign does not take card or UPI credentials and does not process payments. The parking charge is settled at the spot, in cash or UPI, as the owner and vehicle owner agree.

FAQ

Questions people ask

How do I get the app?

Zlign is in closed testing on Google Play, so it is not public yet. Use the testing link with a Google account that has been added as a tester, accept the invite, then install from Play. Testers can sign in to the app with their own Google or email account.

How much does it cost?

No price is published for Zlign yet. The app opens, lists and books without any in-app payment screen — the parking charge is agreed with the space owner and paid at the spot.

Is my data private?

Your user profile is readable only by you and by admins. Contact details are shared through the booking, not on a public profile, and reviews are limited to one per completed booking from the vehicle owner.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment. Zlign is an Android app built with Flutter, in closed testing on Google Play. This page will say so if that changes.

Which cities are covered?

Zlign is not limited to a published city list — the map shows what owners have listed. Areas fill up as owners add spaces, so if your area looks empty, it means nobody has listed a bay there yet.
